Every millionaire is just someone who figured out what people will pay for.
Most people think rich people got lucky, or started with money. But look closer, and almost every fortune starts the same way: one person noticed what others wanted, and charged for it.
Imagine two people with the same skills. One spends a year building what he thinks is cool. The other spends that year listening: reading reviews, asking questions, watching what people actually buy.
Twelve months later, the first one has a product nobody asked for. The second one has a waiting list.
This happens because money doesn't go to the smartest person in the room. It goes to the person who understands the people in the room.
Of course, understanding buyers won't make you rich overnight. But it's the one skill every rich person has, and school never teaches it.
Learn why people buy, and you'll never have to wonder how to make money again.
