Most of your lost sales didn't go to a competitor. They went nowhere.
The uncomfortable truth about selling is that your real competition is the buyer doing nothing.
Doing nothing is free. It's familiar. And it can never go wrong.
Buying, on the other hand, comes with risk. Wasted money, wasted time, and feeling stupid if it doesn't work.
So every buyer runs a quiet test. Is staying the same more painful than changing?
If the answer is no, they don't say no. They say, maybe later. And they never come back.
Now imagine two sellers with the same product.
The first one tries to win by adding more. More features, more bonuses, bigger discounts. But more never tips the scale, because more is just more to doubt.
The second one takes risk away. Proof that it works. A clear picture of what changes. And an easy way out if it doesn't.
Same product. But only one of them feels safe to say yes to.
If you remember one thing, remember this. People don't buy the best product. They buy the one that feels safest.
