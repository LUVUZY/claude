Nobody buys a product. They buy the person they become after owning it.
Most sellers list features: the size, the speed, the materials. Then they wonder why nobody cares.
Imagine two people selling the exact same running shoes. One says: lightweight foam, breathable mesh. The other says: this is what you wear the morning you finally become a runner.
The first one gets compared on price. The second one gets bought.
This happens because people don't spend money on things. They spend it on getting closer to who they want to be: richer, fitter, respected, finally taken seriously.
That's why a gym sells the summer body, not the treadmill. And a course sells the new life, not the videos.
Once you see it, every sale changes. You stop asking what your product does, and start asking who your buyer wants to become.
Of course, the product still has to deliver. Promise a new identity and fail, and they will never buy from you again.
Sell the future version of your buyer, and they'll gladly pay for it today.
