If you hang around two millionaires, you will become the third.
Most people think wealth comes from income, talent, or luck. Almost nobody counts the people around them.
But in 2022, economist Raj Chetty studied twenty-one billion Facebook friendships between seventy-two million people. The strongest predictor of who climbed out of poverty was simple. Having friends with money.
His team estimated that if poor kids had as many rich friends as rich kids do, they'd earn about twenty percent more as adults.
This happens because habits spread like a virus. In the Framingham Heart Study, when a friend became obese, your own chances jumped fifty-seven percent. Money works the same way. You copy what you see every day.
Sit with people who talk about what to buy, and you'll spend. Sit with people who talk about what others will pay for, and you'll start selling.
Of course, they won't hand you their money. You still have to do the work.
But you'll stop guessing what rich looks like. You'll see it every day.
