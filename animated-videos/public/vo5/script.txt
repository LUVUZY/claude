If you want people to buy from you, stop being obsessed with your product.
Here's what nobody tells you about selling. The first months usually sell nothing.
You give it everything and get zero sales, and what you're obsessed with decides whether you make it through.
Picture someone obsessed with their product. Every week without a sale feels like proof it's broken, so they add another feature. Then another. Until their motivation runs out.
Now picture someone obsessed with their buyer. They read every review, every complaint, every "not for me." Every conversation teaches them something, so even with zero sales, they get better every single day.
A year later, one of them has rebuilt their product ten times. The other knows their buyer better than anyone, and customers always find the people who understand them.
That's because being obsessed with the buyer gives you the two things selling actually takes: knowing the real problem, and saying it in their words.
Of course, the product still has to work. A great pitch for a bad product just sells refunds.
But if you remember one thing, remember this. Sales are what you get for understanding people. So obsess over the buyer.
